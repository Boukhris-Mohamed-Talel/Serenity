How access works

We keep healthcare-adjacent content clear: you preview first, then request access. Staff confirmation unlocks the full experience—no online payment in this demo.

  1. Preview — Try a short sample (audio, video, or reading) when available.
  2. Request — Submit a cart or a digital access request with your details.
  3. Confirmation — Our team reviews and marks your order confirmed when ready.
  4. Where to find content — Open the product again after confirmation; full media and guidance appear here in the app.

Typical confirmation: 1–3 business days (varies by item).
